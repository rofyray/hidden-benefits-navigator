## The effective-period gap (carried in from the PRD, not resolved)

The task asked that the income table's effective period be resolved before use. It could
not be: the Texas page publishes the table with no effective dates, and the federal page
that would date it returns 403 both to a direct request and to a real headless-Chromium
session. What is known is recorded rather than guessed:

- `src.wic.apply.effectiveFrom` / `effectiveTo` stay `null`.
- `ev.wic.income_table_undated` states the 403 and the site footer's "last updated
  September 17, 2026" and is cited by the income rule itself.
- The limitation appears in `limitations[0]`, and `reviewDueOn` is shortened to
  2026-12-19 so the figures are re-checked sooner than the usual annual cycle.

The figures are still used for screening, because they are published by the administering
state agency on its own application page — but the card carries the caveat, and the label
language is unchanged ("the program makes the final decision").

## Pathways kept separate (acceptance criterion)

- **Category** — `rule.wic.category` is an `any` over pregnant / postpartum under 6 months /
  breastfeeding under 12 months / child under 5. Tests assert none of these member rules
  reads an income or `receives*` field, and all block a strong match when unknown.
- **Income** — `rule.wic.income_table` reads gross monthly income against
  `wic.applicableHouseholdSize` (the pregnancy-adjusted size the page describes), with
  `rule.wic.categorical_income` (Medicaid / SNAP / TANF) as an exception, not a category.

## No agency determination (acceptance criterion)

- Nothing infers pregnancy: every category fact is stated by the person, and an unstated
  category yields "possibly", never "likely" (asserted by a fixture test).
- The nutrition assessment stays a clinic step: `rule.wic.clinic_assessment_referral` has
  effect `referral` with reason code `nutrition_assessment_done_at_clinic`, and no rule
  screens nutritional risk.
- Document qualifiers: income proof is conditioned on `rule.wic.income_table`, benefit
  proof on `rule.wic.categorical_income`, and one `askAgency` item defers to the clinic.
