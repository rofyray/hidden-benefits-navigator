## Acceptance criterion — prediction is never enrollment

`rule.lifeline.participation` is an `any` over `receivesSnap`, `receivesMedicaid`,
`receivesSsi`, `receivesHousingAid`, `receivesVeteransPension` — all stated facts, all with
`blocksLikelyWhenUnknown: true`, each citing `ev.lifeline.participation_is_enrollment`. No
rule reads another program's screening result, and nothing in the catalog can. The limit is
stated first in `limitations`, and a fixture pins a person this app would flag for SNAP but
who is not enrolled to a non-`likely` label.

## Acceptance criterion — verified Texas route

`app.lifeline.texas` → https://www.texaslifeline.org/ (cited to the USAC page that publishes
it), plus `rule.lifeline.texas_route_referral` and an `askAgency` document that says Texas
runs its own sign-up and will say what it needs. National fallbacks: how-to-apply and
companies-near-me.

## Other encoded behaviour

- One benefit per household: `rule.lifeline.one_per_household` on `existingLifeline`, effect
  `exclusion`.
- Economic household: `rule.lifeline.household_definition_referral` is an exception on the
  income rule — the program defines the household, not the app.
- Survivor and Tribal pathways: referral-only rules, never screened, no invented criteria.
- Value copy is service-specific: up to $9.25/month internet or bundled, up to $5.25/month
  voice-only, up to $34.25/month on qualifying Tribal lands — all phrased as "up to".
- Proof options are conditional: program proof on the participation rule, income proof on
  the income rule, identity details always, Texas specifics deferred to the agency.
