## Acceptance criteria

- Every enabled criterion and action carries evidence ids resolving to dated sources — enforced by the catalog validator (`bun run data:validate`, exit 0).
- Older-adult/disability and unknown-deduction paths cannot cause a false definitive exclusion: `rule.snap.net100` has `blocksLikelyWhenUnknown: false`, and `rule.snap.gross130` lists both the 165% path and the older-or-disabled path as exceptions. Asserted by test.
- Partial screen vs. agency determination is stated in `coverage`, `limitations`, `eligibilitySummary` and the fallback explanation.
- No unsupported amounts or obligations: the only document item is "Ask what information applies to your household" with requiredness `askAgency`; no `required` document is claimed. Asserted by test.

## Honest limits recorded

- The SNAP overview page returned 403, so no required-paperwork list is encoded. Recorded in the source record (`reviewStatus: "inaccessible"`) and in evidence `ev.snap.ask_agency`.
- Limits are adjusted annually on October 1; `reviewDueOn` is 2026-10-01 and evidence `ev.snap.transition` states this.
- Deduction amounts are recorded as evidence but the budget is not calculated; the screen does not produce an allotment figure.
