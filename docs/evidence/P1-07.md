## Separation of the two pathways

- Rule-level: a test walks every rule and fails if one cites both `ev.medicare.msp*` and `ev.medicare.extra_help*` evidence.
- Evidence-level: tests assert the MSP evidence text carries no Extra Help figure and vice versa, and that the Extra Help record states its figures are annual.
- Period-level: every MSP threshold is `monthly`, every Extra Help threshold is `annual`, asserted per rule.
- Having an MSP appears only as an Extra Help automatic pathway (`rule.medicare.extra_help_has_msp`), never as an MSP result.
- Each pathway keeps its own destination: the state route for MSP, Medicare.gov and Social Security for Extra Help.

## Acceptance

- **Age alone does not decide enrollment** — `rule.medicare.enrolled` reads `medicarePartA`; no rule references `ageBand` and it is not a required field. Fixture `medicare.younger_not_enrolled` is a 52-year-old with low income whose deciding rule is enrollment, not age.
- **Unknown resources remain unknown** — both resource rules set `blocksLikelyWhenUnknown: true`; fixture `medicare.msp_resources_unknown` expects `possibly` with the resource field listed as missing.
- **No cross-applied claim or value** — enforced by the separation tests above; the value statement names what each pathway covers and says they are decided separately.

## Honest limits recorded

Federal limits are a floor: every income and resource rule carries `rule.medicare.msp_state_variation_referral` as an exception, because Medicare states people may qualify even above the listed figures. QDWI and other working-disability pathways are referred rather than scored. The card does not compute an award amount.
